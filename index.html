How AI Video Detection Operates

Modern generative AI videos are convincing to the naked human eye, but leave distinct mathematical fingerprints.

1. Spatio-Temporal Consistency

Video diffusion models predict frames sequentially or in 3D latent spaces. Micro-inconsistencies arise between frames, causing background objects to subtly morph or flicker when tracked across optical flow vectors.

2. Sensor Noise & PRNU Profiling

Every real optical camera sensor has Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU) and Poisson-Gaussian sensor noise. AI generated videos are generated from smooth mathematical distributions and lack genuine optical sensor noise.

3. High-Frequency Fourier (FFT)

Convolutional and deconvolutional upsampling in generative networks creates distinct periodic checkerboard patterns and high-frequency spectral roll-offs visible only in 2D Discrete Fourier Transforms.

4. Multimodal Audio-Visual Sync

Synthetic voices generated by neural TTS models often clip sharply above 16kHz or lack natural breathing phonetics, while synthesized mouth lips suffer from 40ms to 120ms temporal phase lags.
